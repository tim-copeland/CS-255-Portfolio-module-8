# CS-255-Portfolio-module-8
Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?
DriverPass case study is about the design of a complete management system for the driving school. The client needed to create a centralized platform where he could manage all activities such as registering of students, booking lessons, scheduling of instructors, and managing payments. The idea was to automate all activities and ensure that everything can be handled automatically by the system.

What did you do particularly well?
As for what I feel I excelled at the most in, I would say that my Requirements Gathering was effective. By being able to distinguish between functional and non-functional requirements, technical requirements such as data protection were considered alongside user requirements such as a good interface for making bookings.

If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?
The area that needs to be improved would be the UML Class Diagrams found in the System Design Document.

How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?
Through the process of analyzing the user requirements, I came up with Use Case Diagrams and User Stories to give a clearer picture of how the user would work with the software application. This step is extremely important, since there could be cases whereby even though the system is excellent in itself, the users won’t be able to use it due to some flaws that arise from lack of proper analysis of their requirements.

How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?
Before I started implementing the design of the software, I should spend considerable time analyzing the requirements. I intend to implement an Agile prototype and iterative design approach in the future. Using the iterative design process, I can develop smaller, working prototypes and ensure that my final design meets the needs of the customer.
